Climate Fast Facts (Lagos Climate)

🌊 High Relative Humidity

Lagos maintains an average humidity of around 80% year-round. This causes weather to feel much warmer (elevated feels-like index) than the standard ambient thermometer readings.

💨 The Harmattan Phenomenon

Between December and February, dry, dusty trade winds blow from the Sahara Desert, dropping humidity levels dramatically and causing haze conditions over Lagos.
